# Revive Initiative — Brand Guidelines (v1)

## Logo
- `logo.png` — full-color logo on white/light backgrounds.
- `logo-dark.png` — full-color logo on black/dark backgrounds (use in footers, dark sections).
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the leaf mark. Do not stretch, recolor, or rotate.

## Tagline
"From a drop to a lifeline."

## Colors
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Blue | #1B4965 | Headings, primary text on light backgrounds, footer background |
| Fresh Green | #5CBE5A | Accents, icons, progress bars |
| Fresh Green Dark | #3F9142 | Buttons, links on hover |
| White | #FFFFFF | Backgrounds |
| Light Gray | #F2F4F5 | Section backgrounds, cards |

Avoid red. Favor generous white space, rounded corners (1rem+), and soft shadows over hard borders.

## Type
- Display / headings: Lora (serif)
- Body: Inter (sans-serif)

## Voice
Professional, human, transparent, hopeful. No sensationalism, no political language, no emotional manipulation.
